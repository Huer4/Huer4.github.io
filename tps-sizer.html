PROJECT NOTES

Preliminary sizing tool

The objective

Translate a local thermal history and protected-body temperature limit into an initial alumina-blanket thickness and mass estimate.

The approach

The solver forms an equivalent square-temperature pulse and applies the Eq. 35 sizing method described in NASA/TP-2012-217595. A separate eigenfunction-series check evaluates the delayed peak temperature. The GUI extends the workflow to multiple axial stations and exports reports.

The outcome

In the supplied four-station temperature-history demonstration, estimated thicknesses range from 50.85 to 79.52 mm. The total blanket mass is 6.29 kg over 1 m², and the simplified series-check peak stays below the 350 K limit at each station.

Scope & interpretation

These are synthetic demonstration results with illustrative material properties and protected-body heat capacity. Regions are independent one-dimensional models. This tool sizes insulation, not ablators, and does not establish a flight-qualified design.
